GAIT

GobiArts Association of Information Technology

  • GAIT is a premier association formed by the Department of Computer Science to bridge the gap between academia and industry.
  • It serves as a professional forum through which students enhance their leadership qualities and organizational abilities.
  • The association regularly organizes Guest Lectures, Workshops, and Seminars featuring industry experts.
  • GAIT is the driving force behind two major annual events: QUTRIX (Intercollegiate) and GAITFEST (Intracollegiate).

Intercollegiate meet (QUTRIX)

It is the annual technical extravaganza and state-level technical symposium conducted every year during the month of September.

QUTRIX invites Computer Science students from various Institutions and Universities across Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry. This platform empowers participants to improve communication, demonstrate spirit of teamwork, and master organizational skills through high-stakes competition.

Intra Collegiate meet (GAITFEST)

A vibrant internal platform designed for students to demonstrate their technical powers and engage in IT-related knowledge sharing.

  • Core Focus: Paper Presentation, Technical Quiz, and Word Hunt.
  • Skill Development: Web Design, Software Contests, and Tech Marketing.
  • Community: Encourages project showcases and tech talks within the department.
