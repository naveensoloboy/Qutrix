General Rules

  • No Registration Fee.
  • Participant certificate will be provided.
  • Participants should bring their Student ID Card (Must) and Bonafide Certificate(Optional).
  • Electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the event area.
  • Judges' decision will be final and binding.
  • The participating members may vary depending upon the events.
  • Each participant should participate in only TWO events.
  • Word Hunt Prelims and Software Contest Prelims will take place simultaneously.
  • Web Design Prelims and Quiz Prelims will take place simultaneously.
  • Dance participants should participate only in Dance and Paper Presentation. They are not allowed to participate in any other events.
  • After registration, participants will be redirected to a WhatsApp group link page. Participants are instructed to join their respective event WhatsApp group for further details and queries.
