PAPER PRESENTATION :
  • One Team Per Department.
  • Maximum of Three Participants per team (Equal participation is must).
  • The paper must be IEEE format.
  • Full paper of minimum 6 pages with an abstract, not exceeding 250 words along with Hard Copies and One Soft copy through mail (qutrix2k25ppt@gmail.com) should be sent.
  • Title, Participants Name, Mobile Number, E-mail id, College name (for further communication) Abstract in a separate sheet.
  • Paper Size :A4, Font Name: Times New Roman, Font Size: Heading-14, Sub Heading: 12+BOLD, Paragraph -12, Line spacing -1.5
  • Paper should reach us on or before 5th September, 2025 along with a Bonafide Certificate
  • Selected teams will be informed through e-mail on 9th September 2025. Selected team should participate in the event and should give a Power Point Presentation at allocated time
